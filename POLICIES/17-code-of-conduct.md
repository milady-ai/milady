# 17 — Code of Conduct / Ethics

**Owner:** People Ops
**Review cadence:** Annual
**SOC2 mapping:** CC1.1, CC1.5

## Purpose

Establish the ethical baseline for employee and contractor behavior.

## Scope

All employees, contractors, advisors, and contributors acting on behalf of Eliza.

## Statements

1. **Integrity** — be honest in code, in commits, in customer communication, and in incident reporting.
2. **Respect** — no harassment, discrimination, or hostile behavior in any company channel or event.
3. **Confidentiality** — customer data and unreleased product details are not shared outside authorized channels.
4. **Conflict of interest** — disclose financial, advisory, or family relationships that could influence Eliza decisions.
5. **No retaliation** — concerns raised in good faith (security, ethics, harassment) do not trigger adverse action.
6. **Customer safety** — when product capability could harm a user (e.g., a plugin doing dangerous operations on their machine), engineering escalates rather than ships silently.
7. **Open source citizenship** — when contributing to or depending on OSS, follow upstream conduct expectations; report security issues responsibly.

## Disciplinary Process

Violations are reviewed by People Ops + Security Lead. Outcomes range from coaching to termination depending on severity. Documented in HR records.

## Evidence

- Signed acknowledgments annually.
- HR records of any disciplinary action.

## Open Items For Human Sign-Off

- Confirm reporting channel (`conduct@elizaos.ai` or equivalent).
